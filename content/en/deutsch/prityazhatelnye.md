# Ihre, seine, unsere: possessive words

From a test: *Bitte prüfen Sie zur Sicherheit … Anschrift und teilen Sie uns mit, falls Änderungen nötig sind.* The right answer is **Ihre**, not *seine* or *unsere*.

## How to choose the word

1. **Who is the owner?** That decides the stem: *mein, dein, sein, ihr, unser, euer, ihr, Ihr*. The letter is polite (*Sie*) and the customer's address is to be checked, so *Ihr-*. Not *sein* ("his"), as it is not about a third person, and not *unser* ("our"), as it is not the publisher's address.
2. **What gender and case does the thing have?** That decides the ending. *Die Anschrift* is feminine, accusative, so *Ihre Anschrift*.

| Gender | Nominativ | Akkusativ |
| --- | --- | --- |
| der (m) | mein Brief | meinen Brief |
| das (n) | mein Haus | mein Haus |
| die (f) | meine Anschrift | meine Anschrift |
| plural | meine Briefe | meine Briefe |

The same for *dein-, sein-, ihr-, unser-, euer-, Ihr-*. The word *Anschrift* means **address** (where a person lives), not "inscription" or "description".
