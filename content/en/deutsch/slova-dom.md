# Words for the home

## Wohnung einrichten

**eine neue Wohnung einrichten** — to furnish a new flat: buy and arrange furniture, hang lamps and curtains.

- *Wir **richten** gerade unsere neue Wohnung **ein**.* — We're furnishing our new flat right now.
- *einrichten* is separable: *ein* goes to the end of the sentence.
- The same verb also means "to set up" (a program, an account), but with *Wohnung* it's always about the home.

## Balcony and flowers

| Word | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| der **Balkonkasten**, die Balkonkästen | balcony flower box |
| die **Blume**, die Blumen | flower (decorative, blooming) |
| die **Pflanze**, die Pflanzen | plant in general (also without flowers: grass, greens, vegetables) |

## pflanzen or bepflanzen

| Verb | Followed by | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **pflanzen** | what you plant | *Ich pflanze **Blumen** in den Kasten.* |
| **bepflanzen** | the place you fill with plants (+ *mit*) | *Ich bepflanze **den Kasten** mit Blumen.* |

- *Die Balkonkästen neu bepflanzen* — to replant the balcony boxes.
- *Neue Blumen in die Balkonkästen pflanzen* — to plant new flowers in the boxes.

Both mean almost the same; the only difference is what plays the role of the object.

## der Schaden — damage

*fotografieren* takes the Akkusativ (whom? what?), so *ein* → *einen*:

- *Bitte fotografieren Sie **den** Schaden.*
- *Ich fotografiere **einen** Schaden am Auto.*

The **n** in *Schaden* is part of the word itself, not an ending.

| Case | Singular | Plural |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Nominativ | der Schaden | die Schäden |
| Akkusativ | den Schaden | die Schäden |
| Dativ | dem Schaden | den Schäden |
| Genitiv | des Schaden**s** | der Schäden |

Only the article changes; the Genitiv singular adds **-s**.
