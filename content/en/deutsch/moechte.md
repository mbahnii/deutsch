# Möchte, wollte, mochte

## There is no word "möchtete"

In standard German **möchtete** does not exist. People sometimes say it in speech, but it counts as a mistake.

## What to use

| Form | Meaning | Tense |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ich **möchte** | I would like (polite) | present |
| ich **wollte** | I wanted | past (of *wollen*) |
| ich **mochte** | I liked | past (of *mögen*) |
| **Möchtest** du …? | Would you like …? | present |

- *Ich **möchte** einen Kaffee.* — I'd like a coffee.
- *Gestern **wollte** ich einen Kaffee.* — Yesterday I wanted a coffee.
- *Als Kind **mochte** ich keinen Kaffee.* — As a child I didn't like coffee.
- ***Möchtest** du mitkommen?* — Would you like to come along?

## Typical mistake

- ❌ *Ich möchtete gestern …* → ✅ *Ich **wollte** gestern …*

The past of "want" is always **wollte**.
