# Raum, Platz, Zimmer, Karten

Words that are easy to mix up in Hören and Lesen tasks.

## Raum, Platz, Zimmer

| Word | Meaning | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| das Zimmer | room (in a flat) | *ein Zimmer mieten* |
| der Raum | space in general, a room as a space | *Es braucht mehr Raum für Radfahrer.* |
| der Platz | place, space for somebody or something | *Mehr Platz für Radfahrer ist wichtig.* |

- *Raum* sounds a little more general and abstract; *Platz* is more common in speech when talking about room for someone or something.
- In a text about roads and cycle paths *Raum* means "space", not "room". Here *Raum* and *Platz* are almost interchangeable.

## Karten: tickets or cards

*Karte* has many meanings, so look at the words around it:

- *Karten für alle Anlässe* (in the stationery department) means **cards**, e.g. greeting cards.
- *Theater-, Konzert- und Veranstaltungskarten* means **tickets**.

In a task with a floor plan look for the department that names exactly what you need. Greeting cards were in *Zeitungen, Zeitschriften, Papeterie, Karten für alle Anlässe* on the lower floor (Untergeschoss), while the *Karten* on the Erdgeschoss are tickets.

## How to practise recognising words by ear

1. First listen to the recording **without the text**.
2. Then check yourself against the transcript.
3. Write down the words you did not understand and repeat them.
