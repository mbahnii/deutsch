# How this site works

Everything useful from my chats with Claude about German is collected here.

## Two sections

| Section | What is inside |
| --- | --- |
| **DTZ — exam** | Exam format, letters, the speaking part, breakdowns of exam tasks |
| **German language** | Grammar, words and phrases, homework |

## How to add something new

1. Ask Claude something in an ordinary chat, as before.
2. In Claude Code say: **"обработай немецкий"** (process German).
3. Claude goes through the new chats, adds everything here, publishes the site and deletes the processed chats.

> The site is in Ukrainian by default. Switch the language with the UA / RU / EN buttons at the top. German examples are not translated.

## Handy features

- **Search** at the top looks through all pages at once.
- On the right there is a table of contents for the page.
- The sun button switches the dark theme.
