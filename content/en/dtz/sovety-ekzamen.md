# DTZ exam tips

Tips from the course teachers collected in one place to reread before the exam.

## Bogen — the answer sheet

- Check that the task number in the booklet matches the number on the sheet.
- Mark skipped tasks and at the end check there are no empty fields.
- If you change an answer, fill in the old box completely and mark the new one.
- **Only the sheet** is marked.
- Write only the surname in Name and only the first name in Vorname. Fill in the date of birth.

## Hören — listening

For practice: first listen to the recording without the text, then check against the transcript and write down unfamiliar words ([Raum, Platz, Karten](#/deutsch/raum-platz-karten)). Read the question before the recording and watch what is asked: person, reason, time. If several times are named, link each one to its event (start of the course, registration deadline).

## Lesen — reading

Find the place in the text that confirms the answer. Watch the words *ab, bis, nur, kein*. In adverts all the conditions must match (for example the job and the working hours).

## Schreiben — writing

Mark all the Leitpunkte and reread the task at the end. In an enquiry to a language school you need the language you want and your goal, class times, price and the right level. Details: [Letter rules](#/dtz/pisma-pravila).

## Sprechen — speaking

Add a reason or an example to short answers. When describing a photo, first say what you see. In joint planning react to your partner's suggestion and sum up at the end. Details: [Teil 2](#/dtz/sprechen-teil2), [Teil 3](#/dtz/sprechen-teil3).

## What if you turn the page before you are allowed

This is not from official documents but general exam practice. Check the exact rules of your school or centre in the instructions before the exam.

- If it was an accident and you turned the page back at once, most likely you only get a remark.
- Opening the tasks before the signal breaks exam order. The centre decides the consequences: from a warning to the part being marked as failed, in serious cases exclusion.
- If you already turned the page: do not read on, turn it back, raise your hand and honestly tell the supervisor it was an accident.
