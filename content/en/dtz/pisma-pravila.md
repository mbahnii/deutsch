# Letter rules: how to get 20 out of 20

Everything that repeats in every DTZ letter: style, greeting, structure, marking criteria and the final check. Ready-made phrases are on [Phrases for letters](#/dtz/pisma-redemittel), samples on [8 model letters](#/dtz/musterbriefe).

## Du or Sie

- If the task says "Herr/Frau + surname", always use **Sie**, even for neighbours and colleagues.
- If there is only a first name (e.g. "Ihr Bekannter Max"), use **du**.
- Pick one style and keep it to the end. Mixing du and Sie costs points in two criteria at once.

## Greeting and closing

| To whom | Greeting | Closing |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Unknown person (office, company) | Sehr geehrte Damen und Herren, | Mit freundlichen Grüßen + Vorname Nachname |
| Known name (doctor, boss, landlord) | Sehr geehrter Herr / Sehr geehrte Frau + Nachname, | Mit freundlichen Grüßen + Vorname Nachname |
| Known personally, but on Sie (neighbour, colleague) | Liebe Frau / Lieber Herr + Nachname, | Viele Grüße / Freundliche Grüße + Vorname Nachname |
| Friend, family (first name only) | Lieber / Liebe + Vorname, | Viele Grüße / Liebe Grüße + Vorname |

## Four formatting rules

1. A comma follows the greeting.
2. The first sentence starts with a small letter: *ich schreibe Ihnen, weil …*
3. **Sie, Ihnen, Ihr, Ihre, Ihren** are always capitalised, throughout the letter.
4. No punctuation after the closing; the name goes on the next line.

## Structure of the letter

Greeting → introduction (reason for writing) → **one paragraph for each of the 4 points, in the same order** → closing remark → sign-off.

- 100–150 words are enough. Give each point 2–3 sentences with details; one sentence is too little.
- Not "I write about tools", but "I need a watering can and garden shears, where are they?"
- For openings, requests, refusals, complaints and endings use the ready phrases on [Phrases for letters](#/dtz/pisma-redemittel).

## What you need for 20 out of 20

There are 4 criteria worth 5 points each. Mistakes are allowed as long as the meaning stays clear. You do not need a perfect text, but a complete, polite, logical one with almost no gross errors.

| Criterion | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| I. Task fulfilment | All 4 points covered precisely and concretely, each in its own paragraph. A little invention is fine, but stay on topic. |
| II. Communicative design | Greeting and closing in one style, introduction and ending, varied connectors (weil, deshalb, außerdem, trotzdem, wenn, obwohl), polite phrases (Könnten Sie…, Es wäre nett, wenn…). |
| III. Grammar | Use constructions you are sure of: one correct complex sentence beats three wrong ones. Leave 3–5 minutes to check. |
| IV. Vocabulary | Do not repeat the same words, use synonyms (kaputt / defekt, sagen / mitteilen). Exact words for the topic: Heizkosten, Schimmel, Gießkanne. |

## Typical mistakes that cost points

- Mixing styles: *Ich wende mich an Sie* in a letter to a friend on du. This phrase is for formal letters only.
- Word order in subordinate clauses: verb at the end, subject right after the conjunction: *…und es bald Winter wird*. See [Word order](#/deutsch/pridatochnye).
- Cases: *deinen Briefkasten* (masculine accusative).
- Verb patterns: *bitten, … zu …* (no *um*), *sich freuen auf / über*, *sich ändern*. See [Verbs with prepositions](#/deutsch/glagoly-s-predlogami).
- Points covered too briefly: one sentence per point instead of 2–3.
- On the form: only the surname in Name, only the first name in Vorname; fill in the date of birth.

## Two templates

### Formal letter (Sie)

> Sehr geehrte Frau … / Sehr geehrter Herr …,
>
> ich wende mich an Sie, weil …
>
> …
>
> Ich möchte Sie bitten, … zu …  
> Es wäre sehr nett, wenn Sie … könnten.  
> Falls nichts passiert, werde ich …
>
> Ich freue mich auf Ihre Antwort.
>
> Mit freundlichen Grüßen  
> [Vorname Nachname]

### Informal letter (du)

> Hallo [Name], / Lieber [Name],
>
> danke für deine E-Mail! Wie geht es dir?
>
> …
>
> Kannst du mir bitte sagen, …?
>
> Ich freue mich auf deine Antwort.
>
> Liebe Grüße  
> [Vorname]

## A 30-minute plan

1. 2–3 minutes: choose the task, mark it on the sheet, jot 2 ideas for each point.
2. 20 minutes: write (introduction, 4 paragraphs, ending).
3. 5 minutes to check:
   - the same du or Sie everywhere?
   - verb at the end in subordinate clauses (weil, dass, wenn, ob)?
   - cases after den / einen / deinen?
   - all 4 points covered?
   - greeting and closing present?
